How ordering works

  1. Send an order request from any product page. No payment is taken on the site.
  2. We check the supplier's live price and stock and confirm your size, then email you a secure payment link.
  3. After you pay, we order the vest from the supplier and it ships directly from them to your address. Packaging may show the supplier's name.
  4. We email your tracking number as soon as the supplier provides it.

Returns and exchanges: email us and we'll arrange it under the supplier's return policy (for example, if the size is wrong).

Shipping: US addresses only. We include the supplier's delivery estimate with your payment link.
