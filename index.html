Weighted vest or pressure wrap?

A pressure wrap (ThunderShirt, Mellow Shirt) hugs the whole torso. A weighted vest (Canada Pooch) adds a light insert across the shoulders. Many dogs do well with either; if you're unsure, start with a wrap.
